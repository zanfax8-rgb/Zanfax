Our Services

Complete waterproofing solutions for every structure

Terrace Waterproofing
Advanced PU coating solutions that prevent seepage and protect terraces from long-term water damage.
Bathroom Leakage Repair
Expert leakage detection and repair to keep bathrooms dry, safe and hygienic.
Basement Injection Grouting
High-pressure grouting that stops water ingress and strengthens below-ground structures.
Water Tank Waterproofing
100% watertight tanks using food-grade, long-lasting waterproofing systems.
Brick Bat Coba Waterproofing
Traditional, proven brick bat coba treatment for strong, durable terrace protection.
PU Coating Waterproofing
Seamless coating for terraces and balconies with excellent elasticity and durability.
Crack Injection Treatment
Permanent crack sealing using advanced epoxy injection techniques.
Bitumen Membrane Waterproofing
High-performance membrane systems for roofs and foundations needing long-term protection.
External Wall Leakage Repair
Protects walls from water penetration and extends the life of your structure.
How We Work

Our Process

A systematic six-step approach to complete protection

01
Site Inspection
We inspect the site to understand the structure, problem areas and exact requirements.
02
Leakage Analysis
Our experts identify the source and cause of leakage using proven diagnostic methods.
03
Surface Preparation
We clean and prepare the surface to ensure maximum bonding and lasting performance.
04
Waterproofing Treatment
Premium quality systems are applied, matched to your structure and environment.
05
Quality Testing
Water ponding and quality checks confirm zero leakage before handover.
06
Final Protection Layer
A final protective coating is applied for long-term durability against water and weather.
